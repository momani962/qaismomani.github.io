INTRODUCTION

About Me

I am a first-year CDT student in Robotics and AI for Net Zero at the University of Manchester, currently undertaking an MSc in Robotics following a First Class MEng in Mechatronic Engineering.

My background includes autonomous robotic systems, manipulation, embedded systems, instrumentation and multidisciplinary engineering projects. Through academic projects and research experience, I have developed an interest in the complete lifecycle of robotic systems, from initial requirements and design through to integration, testing and deployment.

My current research direction focuses on robotic inspection in confined steel and concrete structures, contributing towards safer and more effective inspection and maintenance in challenging environments.

RESEARCH DIRECTION

Research Interests & Career Objectives

Inspection Robotics

Robotic platforms for inspection and maintenance, particularly where conventional access is difficult, hazardous or inefficient.

Extreme Environments

Autonomous and remotely operated systems for confined, inaccessible and challenging environments, including applications in the nuclear sector.

Robotic Systems Engineering

The design, integration and validation of robotic platforms combining mechanical, electronic, control and software subsystems.

Robotics for Net Zero

Applying robotics to improve infrastructure inspection, maintenance, operational safety and the sustainability of engineering systems.

My longer-term ambition is to contribute to the research and development of robotic inspection systems through industrial or government-related engineering programmes, with an interest in pursuing further academic research and teaching later in my career.

SELECTED WORK

Engineering Projects

A selection of academic, research and team projects demonstrating my experience in robotics, mechatronics, computational modelling and experimental engineering.

SAPLiNG — Autonomous Litter-Picking Robot

Developed an autonomous litter retrieval system as part of a multidisciplinary robotics team. Led the retrieval subsystem, integrating grasp pose generation, ROS 2 manipulation and MoveIt 2, while also taking responsibility for system-wide power management and supporting hardware integration.

ROS 2 MoveIt 2 Python C++
GitHub Repository Demo Video

ESP — Embedded Systems Buggy

Led the electromechanical design of a line-following robotic buggy, including motor characterisation, transmission selection and a multi-layer chassis designed in SolidWorks. Supported hardware assembly, electrical troubleshooting and PID controller testing.

SolidWorks Embedded Systems PID Control
Project Report GitHub Repository

ARIA — Medical Imaging Modelling

Undertook an individual research project investigating amyloid-related imaging abnormalities. Developed computational methods in MATLAB to introduce synthetic oedema and haemorrhage representations into a digital human brain phantom, supporting the generation of medical imaging data for future AI research.

MATLAB Image Processing Research
Dissertation Source Code

Nanopore Research — Measurement GUI

During a nanotechnology research internship, developed a MATLAB graphical interface for controlling a Keithley source measure unit during nanopore fabrication experiments. Incorporated voltage control, live current measurements, data visualisation and safety cut-off functionality.

MATLAB Instrumentation GUI Development
Technical Report

MNIST — Handwritten Digit Recognition

Investigated machine learning techniques for handwritten digit classification using the MNIST dataset. Analysed model predictions and visualised learned features to explore the performance and behaviour of image classification methods.

Machine Learning Image Classification Data Visualisation
Project Report Source Code

C-Meter Pro — Portable Capacitance Metre

Designed a low-cost, portable capacitance metre for laboratory and field applications. Developed a system concept using a microcontroller-based charge/discharge circuit, with embedded calibration to minimise measurement errors caused by stray capacitance. The proposed device targeted a measurement resolution of 1 pF, accuracy of ±1%, and production cost below £75.

Sensors Engineering Design
