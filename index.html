INTRODUCTION

About Me

I am a first-year CDT student in Robotics and AI for Net Zero at the University of Manchester, currently undertaking an MSc in Robotics following a First Class MEng in Mechatronic Engineering.

My background includes autonomous robotic systems, manipulation, embedded systems, instrumentation and multidisciplinary engineering projects. Through academic projects and research experience, I have developed an interest in the complete lifecycle of robotic systems, from requirements and design through to integration, testing and deployment.

My current research direction focuses on robotic inspection in confined steel and concrete structures, contributing towards safer and more effective inspection and maintenance in challenging environments.

RESEARCH DIRECTION

Research Interests & Career Objectives

  • Inspection Robotics: Developing robotic platforms for infrastructure inspection and maintenance.
  • Extreme Environments: Robotics for confined, hazardous and difficult-to-access environments.
  • Robotic Systems Engineering: Multidisciplinary design, integration, testing and deployment of robotic systems.
  • Robotics for Net Zero: Applying autonomous technologies to improve infrastructure maintenance, operational safety and sustainability.

Career Objectives

My ambition is to undertake PhD research in robotic inspection and contribute to the development of robotic platforms for industrial and government-related applications, particularly within the nuclear sector. I am also interested in pursuing further academic research and teaching later in my career.

Engineering Projects

SAPLiNG — Autonomous Litter-Picking Robot

Developed an autonomous litter retrieval system as part of a multidisciplinary robotics team. Led the retrieval subsystem, integrating grasp pose generation, ROS 2 manipulation and MoveIt 2, while also managing system-wide power integration and supporting hardware development.

Technologies: ROS 2, MoveIt 2, Python, C++, Robotic Manipulation

ESP — Embedded Systems Buggy

Led the electromechanical design of a line-following robotic buggy, including motor characterisation, transmission selection and the development of a multi-layer chassis in SolidWorks. Supported hardware assembly, electrical troubleshooting and PID controller testing.

ARIA — Medical Imaging Modelling

Undertook an individual research project investigating amyloid-related imaging abnormalities. Developed computational methods in MATLAB to introduce synthetic oedema and haemorrhage representations into a digital human brain phantom, supporting the generation of medical imaging data for future AI research.

Technologies: MATLAB, Image Processing, Computational Modelling, Medical Imaging

Nanopore Research — Measurement GUI

During a nanotechnology research internship, developed a MATLAB graphical interface for controlling a Keithley source measure unit during nanopore fabrication experiments. Incorporated voltage control, live current measurements, data visualisation and safety cut-off functionality.

Technologies: MATLAB, App Designer, Instrumentation, Data Acquisition

MNIST — Handwritten Digit Recognition

Investigated machine learning techniques for handwritten digit classification using the MNIST dataset. Analysed model predictions and visualised learned features to explore the performance and behaviour of image classification methods.

Technologies: Machine Learning, Image Classification, Data Visualisation

Report and source code to be added

C-Meter Pro — Portable Capacitance Metre

Designed a low-cost, portable capacitance metre for laboratory and field applications. Developed a system concept using a microcontroller-based charge/discharge circuit, with embedded calibration to minimise measurement errors caused by stray capacitance. The proposed device targeted a measurement resolution of 1 pF, accuracy of ±1%, and a production cost below £75.

Technologies: Embedded Systems, Instrumentation, Sensors
